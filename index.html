About me

I'm Shabir Ahmad, an independent developer from Loralai, Balochistan. I build Android apps and games with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, and web apps with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I take each project from the first idea to its release on Google Play.

My app Pashtani Wolsi Adab grew out of my love for Pashto folk literature. It collects ټپې and کاکړۍ غاړې so that anyone can read, search and share them, on Android and on iPhone.

Alongside development, I help run my family's agricultural supply business in Loralai, where I look after daily operations and suppliers.

Pashtani Wolsi Adab پښتني ولسي ادب

Live on Google Play

Pashto folk literature app for Android and iPhone

A home for Pashto folk literature. Read ټپې / لنډۍ and کاکړۍ غاړې letter by letter, search them, keep favorites, share any verse as an image, and send new ones for review. Reading works offline.

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Supabase
  • Offline-first
  • iPhone web app
