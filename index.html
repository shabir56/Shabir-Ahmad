I’m Shabir Ahmad, and I run my own agriculture shop in Loralai. Working for myself taught me to handle the whole business: serving farmers, solving problems on the spot, and learning as I go.

Outside the shop, I read folk literature and philosophy, follow science and technology, and look up at the night sky. That curiosity led me to build apps, starting with a small game and a tool that turns web projects into Android apps.
