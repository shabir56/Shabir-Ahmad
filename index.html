Pashtani Wolsi Adab پښتني ولسي ادب

In Development

Pashto folk literature app for Android

Folk literature is a living part of Pashto culture. This native Android app is built to preserve it, make it easy to read, and collect it in one place. It covers two genres, ټپې / لنډۍ and کاکړۍ غاړې, and the whole interface is in Pashto, laid out right-to-left.

Built with

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Material 3
  • Room
