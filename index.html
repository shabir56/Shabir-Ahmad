My name is Shabir Ahmad, born on September 18, 2006, and based in Loralai, Balochistan. I completed my primary education at Jinnah School Loralai, my middle school education at Clever Mind School Loralai, and my matriculation at ABF School Loralai. I subsequently earned my Intermediate in Computer Science (ICS) from Government Degree College Loralai.

Alongside my father, Sherafuddin Shad, I serve as a self-employed professional in the agricultural sector, managing daily operations and commercial supply chains at our shop.

Beyond my agricultural business, I am deeply passionate about Pashto folk literature, philosophy, science, and astronomy. I am also an active web and mobile application developer, creating software solutions and engaging games using modern software development practices.
